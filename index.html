About us

A taste of the island on Barton Road.

The Jerk Grill is a Black-owned Jamaican restaurant in Redlands' Brookside Plaza. Chef-owner Lerone Mullin grew up helping his mother cook on their farm in St. Mary Parish, Jamaica. He started popping up at local breweries just before the pandemic with jerk chicken, festival and rice, built a loyal following, and opened on Barton Road in January 2022.

The menu blends that Jamaican upbringing with Southern California favorites: jerk off the grill, tender oxtail, flaky patties, and the oxtail smash burger that landed us in the Los Angeles Times.

Every plate is made fresh to order. Come for the food and stay for the music: grab a seat inside or on the patio with a cold beer or a house lemonade.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is The Jerk Grill, and where do I park?

We're at 1560 Barton Rd, Floor 1, in Brookside Plaza in Redlands, CA 92373, with parking in the shopping center lot. It gets busy around lunch and dinner, so ordering online ahead of time makes pickup quick.

Who owns The Jerk Grill?

The Jerk Grill is Black-owned. Chef-owner Lerone Mullin grew up in St. Mary Parish, Jamaica, started with brewery pop-ups in 2020, and opened the restaurant on Barton Road in January 2022.

What should I order on my first visit?

Most first-timers go for the Oxtail Smash Burger Meal, the dish the Los Angeles Times featured, or a Meal Combo with your choice of protein, rice, two sides and a drink. Add a Jamaican patty or festival on the side.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes. Veggie patties, cauliflower rasta pasta, tofu rasta pasta and steamed veggies are all on the menu. Ask at the counter and we'll help you build a plate that fits how you eat.

Do you offer catering?

Yes. See our catering menu online or call (909) 735-5400 to plan food for your party, office or event.

Can I order Jamaican patties in bulk?

Yes. Call ahead at (909) 735-5400 for larger patty orders so we can have a fresh batch ready when you arrive.

Do you serve beer?

Yes, we serve cold beer, including Red Stripe, plus our house lemonade and mango passionfruit lemonade.
